How to buy nothing

No wallet connection. No signatures. No app asking for permission. You send a transaction the way you'd send one to a friend.

STEP 1

Decide what nothing is worth

Any amount. There is no minimum and no ceiling. The ledger ranks by what it was worth in dollars at the moment you're reading it.

STEP 2

Write your inscription

Type a line below. Copy the encoded version into your wallet's data field. It rides along inside the transaction. Nothing is stored here.

STEP 3

Send it, then wait

Send to the matching address. Once the block confirms, this window reads your line straight off the chain and puts you in the ledger.
